The Case

Emily Kingstone
is dead.

Popular. Admired. Ruthless beneath the surface. The basketball team captain was found dead in the Society room — and almost everyone who knew her had a reason to want her gone.

You and your team are the investigators. Two rooms. A web of secrets. Physical evidence, digital footprints, and a confession buried somewhere you haven't looked yet.

The question isn't just who did it — it's how far the conspiracy goes.
